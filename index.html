Recommended tools for competitor post data
Socialinsider (~$82/mo) - cross-platform competitor benchmarks with per-post engagement on IG, TikTok, FB. Cleanest paid option. Phlanx - engagement rate only, lighter signal. Modash - influencer-flavoured but exposes per-post engagement on any public IG/TikTok account. Exolyt or Pentos - TikTok-specific with full post-level metrics including trending sounds and hashtag context. No MCP exists for any of these yet, so initial sync would be CSV import; long-term a custom MCP wrapper is feasible.

Notes on this snapshot

Living Things IG estimate is from May 2026 brand research, not verified live. Hip Pop TikTok is materially smaller than IG (3.4k vs 28k) - they're an IG-first brand. More Fizi is the product line, but social presence lives under parent More Nutrition - their TikTok (457k) is one of the largest in the German nutrition category, but the audience is broader than just More Fizi drinkers. SuperPop TikTok confirmed as @drinksuperpop. XOXO TikTok confirmed as @xoxo.soda.
